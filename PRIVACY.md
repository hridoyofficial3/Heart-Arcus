# Arcus – Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 2026_

Arcus is a personal expense-tracking app. This policy explains what data the app handles.

## Data stored on your device
All your entries (expenses, income, loans, notes) are stored locally on your phone. We do not run any server and we do not collect, receive, or sell your data.

## Google Drive backup (optional)
If you choose to connect Google Drive, Arcus asks for:
- **Email and basic profile** – only to show which Google account is connected.
- **Drive "app data" access (drive.appdata)** – lets Arcus save and restore one backup file in a hidden, app-specific folder in your own Google Drive. Arcus cannot see or access any of your other Drive files.

The backup goes directly from your phone to your own Google Drive. It is never sent to the developer or any third party. You can disconnect at any time from the app's settings, and you can delete the backup from your Google Account's Drive storage settings.

## Sharing
We do not share your data with anyone.
